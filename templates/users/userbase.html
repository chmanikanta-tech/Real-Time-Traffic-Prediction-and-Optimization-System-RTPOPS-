📘 About Traffic prediction

Traffic prediction is the process of forecasting future traffic conditions—such as congestion levels, travel time, and vehicle density—based on historical data, real-time sensor input, and machine learning algorithms. It plays a crucial role in intelligent transportation systems by enabling proactive traffic management and decision-making.

⚙️ How It Works
